Internship FAQs

Who can apply?

Students and recent graduates can register for any available internship area.

Is prior experience required?

No. Beginners are welcome. Tell us honestly about any projects, coursework, or tools you have explored.

Which areas are available?

You can choose areas such as software development, data science, marketing, finance, human resources, design, or type another area.

What happens after registration?

You receive a registration ID and downloadable summary. The program team can then use those details for the next step.
